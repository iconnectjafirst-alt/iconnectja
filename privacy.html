4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Jamaica Data Protection Act 2020, we process your personal data based on the following lawful grounds:

  • Contract Performance: To provide our Platform services and fulfil our agreement with you
  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent (e.g., marketing communications)
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our services, prevent fraud, and ensure platform security
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with Jamaican laws and regulations

5. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify Tradesman certifications (HEART Trust/NTA, NSTA)
  • Facilitate job posting and matching
  • Process payments through our payment partner (WiPay)
  • Enable communication between Residents and Tradesmen
  • Send service notifications via WhatsApp, SMS, or email
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
  • Improve our Platform and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Data Sharing

We share your personal data only in the following circumstances:

6.1 With Other Users

  • Residents can see: Tradesman name, profile photo, trade skills, ratings, verified certification status
  • Tradesmen can see: Resident first name, job details, general location (parish)
  • After booking confirmation: Contact details are shared to facilitate service delivery

Note: Your full address and phone number are NEVER shared until a deposit is confirmed.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with strict authentication
  • Database Security: Row-level security policies ensuring users can only access their own data
  • Secure Authentication: Phone OTP verification with rate limiting
  • Payment Security: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through WiPay
  • Regular Security Reviews: Ongoing monitoring and security assessments
Sensitive Data Protection: Tradesman verification documents (HEART certificates, TRN, National ID) are stored securely and are NEVER visible to Residents or the public.

9. Your Rights Under DPA 2020

Under the Jamaica Data Protection Act 2020, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time (where consent is the legal basis)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@iconnectja.net. We will respond within 30 days.
