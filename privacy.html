3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the platform — matching residents with tradesmen, managing job postings and bids
  • To verify identity — Firebase OTP phone verification confirms you are real before you can post or bid
  • To verify tradesman credentials — we manually review HEART/NSTA certificate uploads before approving profiles
  • To process payments — WiPay Jamaica handles all payment processing; we store transaction records only
  • To send notifications — job alerts, bid updates, and payment confirmations via WhatsApp (Twilio)
  • To protect against fraud — we monitor for fake accounts, fraudulent certificates, and suspicious activity
  • To comply with law — we retain records as required by Jamaican law, including for tax purposes
  • To improve the platform — analysing usage patterns to build better features
Legal Basis (DPA 2020): We process your data under the following lawful bases: (a) Contract — to provide the service you signed up for; (b) Legitimate Interests — fraud prevention and platform security; (c) Legal Obligation — tax and regulatory compliance; (d) Consent — for marketing communications (you can withdraw this at any time).

6. Your Rights Under the Data Protection Act 2020

As a Jamaican resident, you have the following rights under the Data Protection Act 2020:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to Object — Object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — Withdraw consent for marketing at any time
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint — Complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC) of Jamaica

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@iconnectja.net or via WhatsApp at +1 (876) 501-5017. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We take data security seriously and have implemented the following protections:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3 — All data in transit is encrypted (Vercel auto-SSL)
  • Phone OTP verification — No passwords that can be stolen; all logins verified by your phone
  • Contact info hidden by default — Your phone number and address are never shown to tradesmen until a deposit is confirmed
  • Row-Level Security — Our database (Supabase PostgreSQL) enforces strict access rules: you can only access your own data
  • Private file storage — HEART/NSTA certificates are stored in a private bucket; they cannot be accessed via a public URL
  • Rate limiting — OTP requests and all API calls are rate-limited to prevent abuse
  • Audit logging — All admin actions are logged with timestamp and IP address

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and the Office of the Information Commissioner within 72 hours as required by DPA 2020.

8. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies and local storage:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the platform to function (session management, security tokens). You cannot opt out of these.
  • Analytics cookies — We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how people use the platform. You may opt out via your browser settings.

We do not use advertising cookies or share cookie data with third-party advertisers.

9. Minors

iConnectJA is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately at hello@iconnectja.net and we will delete the account.
