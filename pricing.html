How the Commission Works

When a resident hires a tradesman and pays a deposit, iConnectJA takes a small platform fee. This covers our verification, matching, and secure payment processing.

The deposit is held in escrow by WiPay Jamaica and released to the tradesman only when the job is marked complete. Your money is always protected.

✓ Residents pay: 8% platform fee added at checkout
✓ Tradesmen receive: full job value minus subscription fee

📋 Example — J$20,000 Plumbing Job

Job value agreed J$20,000
Platform fee (8%) + J$1,600
Resident pays total J$21,600
Tradesman receives J$20,000
Paid within 24 hrs of completion

Common Questions

Is it really free to post a job?

Yes — completely free. Residents pay a platform fee only when they hire a tradesman and confirm a deposit. No credit card needed to post.

When do I get paid as a tradesman?

Within 24 hours of the resident marking the job complete. Funds are held securely in WiPay escrow until then. No waiting weeks for a cheque.

What if I need to cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time. Your profile stays visible until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no runaround.

Do I need a HEART/NSTA certificate?

Yes — all tradesmen on iConnectJA must hold a valid HEART/NSTA certificate in their trade. This is what makes our verification meaningful and protects homeowners.

What happens if there's a dispute?

Raise a dispute within 48 hours of the agreed completion date. iConnectJA reviews both sides and issues a decision within 5 business days. Refunds processed in 3–5 business days.

Is WiPay safe to use?

Yes. WiPay Jamaica is a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. We never store your card details. All transactions are encrypted and secured by WiPay's infrastructure.

Can I operate in multiple parishes?

Yes — your profile can cover multiple parishes within the same subscription. You'll receive job notifications for every parish you select when setting up your profile.

How do I get my first month free?

WhatsApp us to join. The first month is free for all new HEART-certified tradesmen who complete profile verification within 7 days of signing up.
